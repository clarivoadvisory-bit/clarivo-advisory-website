Meet the Founder

Shiv Kumar

Founder & Principal Advisor — Clarivo Advisory

"I don't deliver reports. I deliver working systems."

Shiv Kumar is a senior fintech and global business professional with over 25 years of hands-on experience across financial markets, AI automation, trade intelligence, blockchain platforms, and international business development. Based in Ahmedabad, India, he has built and deployed systems — not just strategies — across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and the United Kingdom.

His career spans VP-level roles at fintech organisations, directorial positions in South-East Asia, regional leadership across the Middle East, and decades of founder-led advisory work. He has developed algorithmic trading systems on MT5/MQL5 covering XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY and Indian equity derivatives (NIFTY options Tuesday expiry 65 lots; SENSEX Thursday expiry 20 lots); led B2B intelligence and export lead platforms; and driven fintech expansion into UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Kenya, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Cambodia and Vietnam.

At Clarivo Advisory, Shiv works directly with exporters, manufacturers, fintech companies, market participants and growth-stage businesses to implement AI automation systems, trade intelligence platforms, financial markets automation and CRM intelligence frameworks — with a focus on measurable execution, not theoretical recommendations.

He is a recognised speaker and academic contributor, having delivered lectures at LJ University, Sardar Vallabhbhai Global University and BK School of Professional & Management Studies on Project Management, Global Business and Derivatives — and a mentor to MBA and AI students on applied AI in business.
